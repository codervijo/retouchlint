# MLS photo rule sources — v1.H primary-source check

The v1.H pages (`/mls-photo-requirements/` and its children) state MLS rules. Every
claim on those pages must trace to a quote below, and every quote below was read
from the MLS's (or its operating association's) own publication. Re-check before
editing a page; MLS rules change (CRMLS revised Rule 11.5.2 on 2026-07-10).

Retrieved 2026-11-08 unless noted. Statute: Bus. & Prof. Code § 10140.8 (AB 723),

## CRMLS — Rules & Policies, effective July 10, 2026

Source: https://go.crmls.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/CRMLS-Rules-Policies-7.10.26_v2.pdf
(linked from https://go.crmls.org/crmls-rules-and-policies/). Pages 26–28.

- **11.5(c) Truthful Representation.** "Media submitted to the MLS must be a truthful
  representation of the property in all respects … Photos depicting views that are not of
  the property or from the property, or that are depicting HOA amenities must be captioned
  as such." Renderings of conditions in place at closing / in the price "may be used in
  accordance with the requirements of Rule 11.5.2"; renderings of conditions not present at
  closing "may be placed in the Supplements" with a notice that they aren't included.
- **11.5(e) Branded Media.** Branding prohibited (signs, contact info, logos, identifiable
  persons, external links, team names…).
- **11.5.1** At least one photo or rendering "accurately depicting a substantial portion of
  the exterior" within two (2) days of entry; Business Opportunity exempt; seller's written
  no-photo instruction to the MLS/AOR within two days.
- **11.5.2 Digitally Altered Images.** "A Subscriber who submits to the MLS any Digitally
  Altered Image must also (i) submit the original, unaltered version of the digitally altered
  image, (ii) ensure that the original, unaltered version is displayed in the listing
  immediately before or after the digitally altered image, and (iii) ensure that the
  digitally altered image is labeled as "Photo Modified", "Virtually Staged", "Digitally
  Altered," or "AI Altered," or some accurate substantially similar term in the text field
  section for the altered photo in the add/edit module."
- **Definition (narrower than the statute).** "… altered through the use of photo editing
  software or artificial intelligence to add, remove, or change elements in the image
  related to furniture or appliances." Excludes "only slightly enhanced lighting, sharpening,
  white balance, color correction, angle, straightening, cropping, exposure, or other common
  photo editing adjustments …".
- **Prohibition.** "Pursuant to this section and CRMLS Rules 11.5(c) and 12.10 regarding
  Truth in Advertising, Subscribers may not add to a listing record any image or photo that
  has been altered to add, remove, or modify any (i) real property elements including but not
  limited to flooring, walls, windows, structural elements, cabinetry, paint color, hardscape,
  landscape, façade, fixtures, or floor plans unless the listed property will be improved to
  match the altered photo at time of close as part of the listed price, or (ii) elements
  outside of, or visible from, the property, including, but not limited to, streetlights,
  utility poles, views through windows, and neighboring properties."
  - Reading: the "unless … improved to match" exception is inside clause (i). Clause (ii)
    states no exception. Sky replacement is not named in either list — pages say so rather
    than guess.
- **11.5.3** MLS/AOR may remove non-compliant media without notice.

## MLSListings — Rule 7.11.2 Listing Photograph Requirements

Source: https://support.mlslistings.com/s/article/AB723---Altered-Images-Overview
(MLSListings Compliance Update; JS-rendered — read via treg.web.extract). Says the MLS
Rules Committee and Board adopted the change "in December" in support of AB 723 (effective
date of the rule not stated beyond that).

- Photo within **1 day** after entry (except Members Only–Do Not Show); front of the building
  for existing buildings; no text-only images; text/overlays only to describe property
  characteristics (boundaries, pointers, physical descriptions).
- "Any enhancements or modifications to images must be disclosed in the public remarks, e.g.
  color enhancement or virtual staging."
- "A Participant or Subscriber who submits any digitally created or altered image must also
  (i) submit the original, unaltered version of the digitally altered image, and (ii) ensure
  that the digitally altered image is marked in a reasonably conspicuous manner as "altered,"
  "digitally altered," "AI altered," or some accurate, substantially similar, term."
  - No before/after sequencing requirement in the quoted rule.
- No-photo listings: MLS-provided "Seller Prefers No Photo" image + written seller
  authorization. New construction: realistic rendering of the front until completed.
- "**Important note:** MLSListings does not allow links of any kind from the photos in a
  listing. QR codes and links may be used in other media, such as print or social media for
  example, but not on the MLS."
- Contact on page: compliance@mlslistings.com, 408-874-0200 option 2.

## Bay East Association of REALTORS® — MLS Rules 11.5.1 / 11.5.2

Source: https://bayeast.org/digitally-altered-mls-photo-rule/ ("New MLS Rules Effective
January 1, 2026"). The page says "the MLS has added two new sections to the MLS Rules and
Regulations"; it does not name the MLS. Pages call it "Bay East's MLS rules" — don't name
bridgeMLS without a source.

- **11.5.1 Definition** — same wording as § 10140.8(b): fixtures, furniture, appliances,
  flooring, walls, paint color, hardscape, landscape, façade, floor plans, and elements
  outside or visible from the property (streetlights, utility poles, views through windows,
  neighboring properties); excludes lighting, sharpening, white balance, color correction,
  angle, straightening, cropping, exposure.
- **11.5.2** "(i) submit the original, unaltered version of the digitally altered image,
  (ii) ensure that the original, unaltered version is displayed immediately **after** the
  digitally altered image, and (iii) ensure that the digitally altered image is marked in a
  reasonably conspicuous manner as "altered," "digitally altered," or "AI altered.""
  - No "substantially similar" clause in the quoted rule text.
- How-to: click "Label" beneath the photo, enter text, Save.
- Bay East's own FAQ suggests labels "Digitally Altered Image", "Virtually Staged", "Digital
  Rendering" and says the label "must clearly disclose that the image has been digitally
  altered." (Tension with the rule's three listed terms — pages point it out, don't resolve it.)
- FAQ: agent and broker responsible regardless of who edited; labels can be added/updated
  any time; non-compliance → MLS compliance violation; agents "encouraged to review active,
  Pending, and Hold status listings."

## SDMLS — AB 723 page (Dec 23, 2025)

Source: https://sdmls.com/ab-723-digitally-altered-images-sdmls-requirements/
High level only; **no rule number or rule text published on the page.** Hub table only, no
leaf page until a rule text is found.

- Effective January 1, 2026; enforced "as part of its MLS rules and data distribution
  standards"; applies to IDX, VOW, API and syndication displays.
- When an altered image is used: "clearly disclosed as digitally altered"; "unaltered original
  image must be available and paired with the altered image"; follow SDMLS labeling/pairing
  instructions (Paragon how-to in their knowledge base).
- Vendors may not remove/obscure altered-image indicators or pairings. Non-compliance "may
  result in SDMLS compliance action or suspension of data access."
- SDMLS lists cropping, straightening, sharpening and noise reduction as "not considered
  digitally altered"; "When in doubt, disclosure is strongly recommended."
