## What we offer (keep the pitch to this; no overclaiming)

`https://retouchlint.com/tools/disclosure-generator/` is free with no sign-up. It
produces copy-paste AB 723 disclosure text in the § 10140.8(a)(1) shape (states that
the image is altered and where the unaltered original can be accessed), in a
per-photo and a listing-wide version. It also tells you when the selected edits are
exempt under § 10140.8(b)(2). The wording was checked against the statute on
2026-09-30. It is not legal advice.

**Do not claim:** that RetouchLint hosts original photos or gives you a durable
public original-photo link (it doesn't yet; see PRD § 6), PDF packets, MLS
integration, or legal review.
